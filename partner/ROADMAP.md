# Roadmap

| Phase | Window | Theme | Exit criteria |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 Foundation | Days 1–14 | Nervous system: auth, intake, ledger, memory | Goal → task list persisted with audit log |
| 1 MVP | Days 15–60 | AI Chief of Staff command center | 10 design partners running weekly goals |
| 2 Launch | Days 61–90 | Paid beta | 25 paying accounts, ≥$5k MRR |
| 3 Workflow expansion | Months 4–6 | Content, sales, support, research engines | 60% weekly retention; 3 engines live |
| 4 Multi-agent autonomy | Months 6–9 | Agent-to-agent handoffs, confidence thresholds | ≥70% of T0/T1 tasks need no human touch |
| 5 Platform | Months 9–15 | API, templates, marketplace | 20 third-party specialists |
| 6 Enterprise | Months 12–18 | RBAC, SSO, compliance, private deploy | First 5 enterprise contracts |

Detailed week-by-week: [90-day plan](docs/launch/90-day-plan.md).
