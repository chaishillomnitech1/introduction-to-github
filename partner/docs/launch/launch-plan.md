# Launch Plan

**Audience:** founders, solo operators, creators, agencies.
**Offer:** "Give Partner one goal; get a governed week of execution."

| Phase | Tactic |
|---|---|
| Pre-launch (wk 1–8) | Waitlist, build-in-public posts, 10 design partners, demo video |
| Launch (wk 11) | Product Hunt, founder communities, email waitlist, agency partner outreach, live demo webinar |
| Post-launch | Weekly case study, template library, referral credit (1 month per referral), onboarding concierge |

**Channels:** X/LinkedIn founder content, communities, agency partnerships, SEO on playbooks.
**Launch KPIs:** 1,000 waitlist, 150 activations, 25 paid, activation <24h for 60%.
**Launch checklist:** pricing page · billing tested · status page · kill switch tested · support inbox · security & privacy page · demo video · press kit.
