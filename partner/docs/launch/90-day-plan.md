## Gates
Day 30: planner+router pass eval ≥80%. Day 60: ≥7/10 partners active weekly. Day 90: ≥$5k MRR, intervention rate down 30% from baseline.

## Risks
Reliability → Sentinel + gates; slow adoption → done-for-you onboarding; cost → model routing and budgets.
