# Brand Package

**Chosen name: Partner** (product: *Partner OS*). Short, trustworthy, human; matches the repo. Sub-brands: **Conductor**, **Sentinel**, **Ledger**, **Specialists**.

## Name alternatives considered
Conductor · Meridian · Axiom OS · Stillwater · Kinetic · Vanguard Ops · Overmind (rejected: ominous) · Flowstate.

## Taglines
- **Primary: Your business, on autonomous mode.**
- The AI operating system for ambitious execution.
- Where strategy becomes action.
- Built to think, assign, and execute.
- Flow at the speed of intelligence.

## Positioning statement
For founders and small teams who are buried in coordination, **Partner** is the autonomous AI operating system that turns outcomes into governed execution. Unlike point AI tools, Partner assigns every task to the right specialist, audits the work, and asks humans only where judgment matters.

## Messaging pillars
Always on · Right specialist, right job · Governed autonomy · Compounding intelligence · Flow.

## Voice
Clear, calm, direct, no fluff. "Flow like water, strike with precision." Say *outcome*, *approve*, *audited*. Avoid hype words ("revolutionary") and mystical jargon.

## Visual identity
Mark: ◈ (diamond within diamond). Palette: Obsidian `#0B0F14`, Slate `#1C2430`, Signal Teal `#19E3C2`, Gold `#E8B84A`, Mist `#E6EDF3`. Type: Inter (UI), JetBrains Mono (code).

## Relationship to Scrollverse
Partner is an independent brand with a fresh identity; it shares the digital-intelligence oversight philosophy but exceeds it with explicit governance and auditing.
