# Investor Narrative

**Partner is a revenue-ready venture asset: a governed autonomous operating layer for businesses.**

**The shift.** Software used to record work; AI now performs it. The unsolved problem is *trust and coordination*: who does what, who checks it, and when a human must decide. Partner owns that layer.

**The product.** Customers state outcomes. Conductor turns them into plans, routes each task to the best-fit Specialist, Sentinel audits quality, risk and brand consistency, and humans approve only what crosses a risk gate. Every action is written to the Ledger.

**Why it wins.** (1) Workflow ownership: once weekly operations run through Partner, switching is costly. (2) Outcome memory: routing improves from real results. (3) Trust infrastructure: audit and approval policy is hard to copy and required by larger buyers. (4) Distribution: templates and shareable outputs pull in collaborators.

**Why it is revenue-ready.** A narrow wedge (AI Chief of Staff for founders/small teams), clear pain, immediate ROI (hours saved), simple pricing, and a services layer that produces cash before the platform matures.

**Unit economics targets.** Gross margin ≥70% after inference; CAC payback <6 months; Pro-to-Team expansion ≥15%; logo churn <4%/mo in year one.

**Honest risks.** Agent reliability (mitigated by Sentinel + gates), inference cost (model routing, caching), incumbents (differentiate on governance and cross-tool orchestration), trust (audit by default).
