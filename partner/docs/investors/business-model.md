# Business Model & Pricing

| Tier | Price | For | Includes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | Trial | 1 workspace, 3 active goals, 50 agent runs/mo |
| Pro | $79/mo | Solo operators, creators | Full roster, memory, 1,000 runs/mo, standard integrations |
| Team | $399/mo (5 seats) | Small teams, agencies | Shared workspaces, approvals, reporting, 6,000 runs/mo |
| Enterprise | Custom (from $2k/mo) | Larger orgs | RBAC, SSO, audit export, private deploy, SLA |

**Other revenue:** usage overage ($0.04/run beyond quota; premium workflows priced per run); Done-for-you onboarding ($2.5k–$15k); premium specialist packs ($29–$99/mo); marketplace take rate 20%; custom Sentinel policies.

**Pricing principle:** price on leverage delivered (hours saved, revenue generated), not raw tokens.

**Illustrative path:** 100 Pro + 30 Team + 5 Enterprise ≈ $7.9k + $12k + $10k = ~$30k MRR (~$360k ARR) plus services.
